# Support FAQ

## Password reset
To reset your password, open the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password", and follow the emailed reset link. The reset link expires after one hour. If the email does not arrive, check spam, then contact support with your account email. Support agents can trigger a new reset email but can never see or set a user's password.

## Deployment status questions
If a customer asks whether a deployment is in progress, point them to the public status page. The status page shows planned deployment windows and maintenance notices. Support agents should not promise exact deployment finish times.

## Billing contacts
Only the account owner can change billing contacts. Support can view the current billing contact but cannot edit it.

## Response times
First response targets: one hour for outages, four hours for account access, one business day for general questions.

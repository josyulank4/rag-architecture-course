# Security and Access Policy

## Access labels
Every document carries one access label: public, internal, or restricted. Retrieval systems must check the label before showing a passage to a user. Restricted passages are never shown to users without the restricted-data role.

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least fourteen characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all staff accounts. Shared accounts are prohibited; every action must be attributable to a named person.

## Data retention
Restricted data is retained for 90 days and then deleted. Internal data is retained for one year. Public help content is retained until replaced by a newer approved version.

## Audit
Access to restricted documents is logged with user, time, and document identifier. Logs are reviewed weekly by the security owner.
